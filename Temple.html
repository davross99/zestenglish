The challenge

One story. 100 gaps. You just have to choose the correct preposition.

As you read Jack's adventure, the story stops at 100 moments where a preposition is missing. Each gap has exactly one correct answer, so you always know why you were right or wrong.

Why AT, IN and ON?

These three words are hard to learn from rules alone. Textbooks give you lists of exceptions; stories give you repetition in context. The more times you meet them in a situation you can picture, the more natural the right choice becomes.
